# Day 29 — Thinking Like a Capacitor

**Goal:** Draw an analogy between thermal systems and electrical capacitors to introduce exponential behaviour.

## 1. Building as a Battery

A capacitor charges quickly at first and slowly as it approaches full charge.  Similarly, a building warms quickly when far from setpoint and slows as it nears setpoint.

## 2. Time Constant (τ)

The **time constant** is how long it takes the system to move ~63% toward its final value.  In HVAC this reflects how fast the zone responds to heating.

## 3. Key Takeaway

Thinking about time constants helps explain why the last degree always takes longer.  This sets the stage for logarithmic modelling.
