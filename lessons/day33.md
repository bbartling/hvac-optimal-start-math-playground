# Day 33 — Comparing Log vs. Quadratic

**Goal:** Compare Model 4 to Model 1 and discuss which situations suit each.

## 1. Shape of the Curve

* **Quadratic:** The time accelerates as ΔT grows; it models heavy zones.
* **Logarithmic:** The time decelerates as ΔT shrinks; it models systems that coast.

## 2. Practical Differences

In a building with slow thermal mass but constant equipment output, Model 1 may be better.  In a system with variable output (like a modulating heat pump), Model 4 may shine.

## 3. Key Takeaway

Choose the model whose shape matches your system’s response curve.
