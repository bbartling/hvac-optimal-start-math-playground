# Day 7 — Week 1 Review: The Limits of Linear

**Goal:** Reflect on the assumptions of Model 0 and understand when it breaks down.

## 1. Where Linear Shines

* Small temperature differences.
* Mild climates with minimal load swings.
* Systems with stable, predictable performance.

## 2. Where Linear Fails

* Deep setbacks: warming a room 10 °F may take much longer per degree than warming 2 °F.
* Extreme weather: heat loss during warm‑up isn’t captured.
* Multi‑stage equipment: capacity changes over time.

## 3. Preparing for Week 2

We’ll introduce a **quadratic model** that captures thermal mass and “heaviness.”  Expect to use algebra and see curves.

## 4. Key Takeaway

Model 0 is simple and works in many cases, but you must know its limitations.  Complex buildings need more advanced models.
